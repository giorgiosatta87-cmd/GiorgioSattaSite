Giorgio Satta is a composer and producer with a broad international background, shaped by hundreds of collaborations across music, gaming and digital media. His work draws on a diverse musical foundation spanning classical music, electronic music and rock, allowing him to move seamlessly between different genres, creative languages and production approaches.

His practice combines musical craftsmanship with contemporary production and sound design, bringing together traditional composition, modern studio techniques and emerging technologies.

His clients and collaborators include Moonton, Lilith Games, Leonardo S.p.A. and ByteDance. He has composed music for productions distributed on Amazon Prime Video and contributed to releases by international labels including Da Vinci Publishing.

Graduating with highest honors, distinction and special mention from the Alessandro Scarlatti Conservatory of Palermo, where he studied Electronic Music and Composition, and with further training in electric guitar at the London College of Music, Giorgio Satta began his career as an international freelancer in 2020.

Since then, he has collaborated with more than 300 clients, including companies, developers, artists and creative studios from around the world, contributing to productions that have reached tens of millions of streams and downloads worldwide.

He has created music and sound for numerous international gaming projects, including major titles such as Mobile Legends: Bang Bang, with over 1.5 billion installations and the world's second-most-watched esport by peak viewership; Dislyte, winner of Best Multiplayer at the 2022 Google Play Best of Awards; as well as Sensory Overload and I-Live.

In the music industry, he regularly works as a ghost producer and audio engineer, collaborating with international artists and labels including gold-certified singer Sasha Strunin, Joey Arias, Prinz, Da Vinci Publishing and Studio Accantus.

His portfolio also includes work in animation, advertising and documentary, including the original score for Skeptico, available on Amazon Prime Video.

He is a Vetted Pro on Fiverr across four different specializations, a distinction awarded to fewer than 1% of applicants.

He has also served as sound engineer for prestigious international events, including the International Rostrum of Composers, and was selected in 2018 for the official concert season of the Teatro Massimo Foundation in Palermo.
